Frequently asked questions

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. You get 3 full mock interviews every month with scores and feedback. No credit card needed.

How does the AI grade my answers?

Each answer is scored 1–5 on accuracy, depth, structure and communication against key points an expert would cover. You see what you covered, what you missed, and how a strong candidate would answer.

What is Voice Discuss?

Talk through any topic out loud with an AI tutor. It answers using the curated videos, papers and blogs in the Learn Hub, cites them, and turns the conversation into study notes when you're done. Voice works best in Chrome and Edge.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Paid plans renew monthly or yearly and you can cancel whenever you like. You keep access until the end of the billing period.

Do you offer student discounts?

Annual plans already save 25%. Colleges and bootcamps can get Teams pricing for their whole cohort.

Which roles do you cover?

Data Analyst, Data Scientist, ML Engineer, AI Engineer and Agent Developer, across beginner, intermediate and advanced levels.
